Scope and assumptions
Constrained rule-based language, fixed overhead camera, known tray coordinates, ideal synthetic colours, and rigid circles/blocks. A* plans a 2D carrying route with conservative circular clearance. The approach is an assumed overhead move; robot joints, contact physics and grip forces are not modelled. Each experiment starts from the selected scene seed. Unseen seeds test new arrangements and combinations, not arbitrary real-world objects. All computation runs locally in Python.
